# Landi

**Your personal AI travel assistant, from the first idea to the journey itself.**

Planning a trip involves more than choosing where to go. Landi brings your ideas, research, itinerary, and practical details together in one place. Share what matters to you, shape a trip that fits, and keep useful information close at hand before and during your journey.

Built for independent travelers, Landi combines AI-assisted itinerary planning, destination research, maps, booking details, and email reminders. Use the desktop workspace to plan and review your trip, then follow along through email as you travel.

## What Landi helps you do

- **Describe your ideal trip.** Capture your destinations, dates, preferences, and practical requirements in a planning workspace.
- **Create a personalized itinerary.** Turn your ideas into a trip blueprint with places, activities, and a day-by-day plan you can review and refine.
- **Research destinations.** Bring together source-backed travel information, local preparation, weather, and transport details to support your decisions.
- **See your trip on a map.** Explore places and routes alongside your itinerary to see how your days fit together.
- **Organize bookings and preparation.** Keep reservation details, travel requirements, and preparation tasks connected to your plans.
- **Review and shape your plans.** Edit an itinerary, revisit its history, and explicitly confirm the version you want to put into action.
- **Stay informed by email.** Receive useful trip information such as daily plans, getting-around and dining guidance, and important updates.
- **Adapt as travel changes.** Landi's travel assistance is designed to consider relevant changes, evaluate alternatives, and keep the itinerary and notifications aligned with the journey.

## Built with MAH

Landi is built and developed with [MAH](https://mah.bot/), a platform for coordinating AI agents and project work. MAH manages the configured AI agents and provides the foundation for Landi's AI-powered planning workflow and its intended ongoing operation.

Landi manages the travel details, itinerary versions, and confirmations that bring your trip together. You can review the planning proposal and itinerary before confirming the specific plan you want to use.

## Getting started

To open and work with this project in MAH, first [download MAH](https://mah.bot/), install it, and configure an AI agent. Then open the Landi project folder in MAH. Visit the [MAH website](https://mah.bot/) for setup information.

## How a trip comes together

1. Open Landi and share your travel requirements.
2. Review the planning proposal and choose to generate a trip blueprint.
3. Explore and refine the itinerary in the workspace, including its places and practical details.
4. Confirm the plan you want to use and follow your journey with email assistance.
