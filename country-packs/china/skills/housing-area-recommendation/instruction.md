# housing-area-recommendation

Produce a preliminary, access-oriented accommodation comparison around a user-selected Plan anchor.

- Explain every candidate using current Amap nearby-place distance facts.
- Keep the first slice to at most five facts and three preliminary recommendations.
- State that nearby-place distance is not a door-to-door commute; ask the user to compare routes before choosing.
- Do not infer live price, inventory, booking, payment, quality, or foreign-guest eligibility.
- Do not claim a “best area” until multiple Plan anchors, route facts, and user preferences are available.
- Every Amap action is an external handoff whose completion stays unknown.
