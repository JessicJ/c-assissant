# Local food guide

Provide destination-level food orientation while a Plan is being prepared.

- Use only normalized facts linked to official-source snapshots whose IDs are in the Country Pack-owned allowlist supplied by Core and whose retrieval time is inside the 30-day freshness window.
- Preserve the Chinese original name and provide an English name and explanation.
- Do not infer that a dish is local, famous, available, suitable, or safe without a supplied source-backed fact.
- Do not choose or schedule a restaurant unless a later user request invokes a separate nearby-place flow.
- Do not propose ordering, booking, payment, or completion actions.
- Degrade explicitly when no traceable local food fact is available.
