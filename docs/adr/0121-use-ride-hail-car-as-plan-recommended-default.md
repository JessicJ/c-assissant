# Use ride-hail car as plan-recommended default

For first-slice Movement Action Cards with `plan_recommended`, Landi will use ride-hail car as the safest default Local Transport Option. Walking may become primary for short daytime acceptable-weather movements, ride-hail motorbike may become primary or alternative for short or medium daytime no-rain no-luggage movements, private driver is mainly a long or cross-area alternative, and scooter rental appears only when rider-confirmed.

## Considered Options

- Prefer the cheapest or most locally common option by default. This may optimize cost or local fit, but it can over-recommend walking or motorbike without enough safety context.
- Use ride-hail car as the default. This is conservative, understandable, and suitable for unknown luggage, weather, or traveler confidence.
