# Render itinerary guide material as rich cards

Landi will render attractions, dining, shopping, and local experiences under the Itinerary workspace as rich planning material rather than simple place rows or one-line recommendations. Cards may use images when suitable images are available, but must render cleanly as text-only cards when they are not. This gives travelers enough context to decide what belongs in their plan, and allows shopping content to describe local products and recommend where to buy them before the traveler adds a related place or shopping block to a day.

An unscheduled Guide Card's default anatomy includes title or local name, short Guide Card Summary, optional image or polished text-only teaser, tags or area when useful, concrete place or related place when applicable, compact Source Marker, and one primary add action. It does not show default day, time, replacement-type label, scheduling rationale, full Guide Value Explanation, comparison tables, or long practical details while it remains outside the Recommended Itinerary.

The shared detail panel carries the richer material: Guide Value Explanation, type-specific comparisons, purchase locations, dining options, experience offerings, practical notes, related material, and source context.

A minimal POI-list presentation was rejected because it would be easier to generate and edit, but would not explain why an item matters or give travelers enough confidence to compose their own itinerary.
