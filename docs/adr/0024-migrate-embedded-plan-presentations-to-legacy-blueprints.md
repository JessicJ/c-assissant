# Migrate embedded Plan presentations to Legacy Trip Blueprints

Historical Plans that embed travel content in `Plan.presentation` are preserved by reconstructing read-only Legacy Trip Blueprints during migration. Landi does not delete them or require fresh Skill research merely to adopt the new boundary; a later traveler-requested change creates a current Trip Blueprint Version before compiling a replacement Draft Plan.

## Consequences

The Plan Workspace supports current and Legacy Blueprint reads during migration. Legacy reconstruction records its source Plan and cannot claim evidence or structure that the historical data did not contain.
