# Require Official Evidence for entry conclusions

Landi marks visa and entry conclusions as verified only when supported by a relevant government, embassy, consulate, immigration authority, or other competent official source. Other travel sources may help discover an issue but cannot establish personal applicability; without Official Evidence, Landi presents the conclusion as requiring verification and directs the traveler to the competent authority.

## Consequences

Evidence authority is evaluated independently from whether a URL is reachable. Entry-related Action Requirements retain their evidence status, retrieval time, and Applicability Inputs so stale or incomplete conclusions cannot silently drive execution. Missing passport or residence details degrade only the personalized entry conclusion; Landi does not infer them or block unrelated Blueprint generation.
