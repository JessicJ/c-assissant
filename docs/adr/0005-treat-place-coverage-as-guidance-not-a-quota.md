# Treat place coverage as guidance, not a quota

Landi does not reject Trip Blueprints using a universal minimum number of map candidates. Place coverage is assessed against the destination, trip duration, and traveler constraints, because a fixed quota such as 55 places for seven days can reward irrelevant padding and reject complete plans for smaller or more focused trips.

## Consequences

Coverage shortfalls may be shown as quality guidance or trigger targeted enrichment, but they do not make an otherwise valid Trip Blueprint fail. Structural validity and evidence status remain independent of candidate volume.
