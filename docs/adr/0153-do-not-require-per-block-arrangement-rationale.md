# Do not require per-block arrangement rationale

Landi may use time, weather, traveler preferences, constraints, fixed bookings, evidence, opening hours, pacing, and route convenience to generate a complete recommended itinerary. Those inputs shape the plan, but the Trip Blueprint Contract and Web workspace should not require an explicit traveler-visible arrangement rationale for every scheduled Itinerary Block.

Timeline blocks should stay focused on the time-ordered plan: time, title, type, place or area, and short status. Details may show guide content, source markers, practical notes, related material, alternatives, and adjustment actions, but they should not depend on a required "why this was placed here" explanation.

Requiring per-block rationale was rejected because it would make the plan presentation feel explanatory and heavy without improving the traveler's main editing job. Constraints that matter operationally should appear as statuses, cautions, source markers, or placement advisories instead.
