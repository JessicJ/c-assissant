# Degrade evidence gaps without rejecting the Trip Blueprint

Landi rejects a Trip Blueprint only for structural defects that make it internally unusable. A place or planning fact without sufficient verified evidence remains visible with a recheck status, because discarding the entire Blueprint would hide useful researched content and force expensive regeneration without improving the uncertain fact.

## Consequences

Structural validation and evidence assessment are separate gates. The interface must expose recheck status, and execution-sensitive decisions may still require later verification even though the Draft Plan remains reviewable. Adjacent scheduled places require a Movement Plan, but unverified mode, duration, route, or geometry degrades that movement to an estimate or recheck state rather than rejecting the Blueprint. Missing reliable coordinates removes Map Eligibility but does not remove the place from written content or invalidate the Blueprint.
