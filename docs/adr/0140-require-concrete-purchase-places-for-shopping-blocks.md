# Separate Local Products from Purchase Places

Landi will model Local Products separately from Purchase Places. A Local Product explains what the traveler may want to buy and why it matters locally. A Purchase Place identifies a concrete place where that product or shopping theme can reasonably be browsed or bought.

One Local Product may link to multiple concrete Purchase Places. When multiple Purchase Places exist, Web should show a Purchase Place Comparison with location, suitable itinerary area, convenience, price or quality signals, hours, source state, and nearby itinerary links when available. A shopping item can become a schedulable Itinerary Block only when the traveler or generated itinerary selects a specific Purchase Place or shopping block, not merely a product category.

Vague purchase recommendations were rejected because they make the guide feel rich while leaving the itinerary unable to answer the practical question of where the traveler should actually go.
