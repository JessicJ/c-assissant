# Separate Local Food from Dining Places

Landi will model Local Food separately from Dining Places. Local Food explains what the traveler may want to eat and why it matters locally. Dining Places identify concrete restaurants, cafes, markets, food halls, food streets, or dining clusters where that food or dining theme can reasonably be eaten or explored.

One Local Food may link to multiple Dining Places. When multiple Dining Places exist, Web should show a Dining Place Comparison with location, suitable itinerary area or meal moment, reservation or queue expectation, dietary fit, price or quality signals, hours, source state, and nearby itinerary links when available.

A dining item can become a schedulable meal block only when the traveler or generated itinerary selects a specific Dining Place or food area, not merely a dish or cuisine idea. Name-only restaurant lists and dish-only recommendations were rejected because they do not help the traveler decide both what to eat and where it fits in the trip.
