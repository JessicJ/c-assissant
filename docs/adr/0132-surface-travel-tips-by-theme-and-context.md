# Surface Travel Tips by theme and context

Landi will organize Travel Tips primarily by theme as Canonical Tips in the Tips area while also surfacing the same underlying tips beside specific itinerary days or blocks as Contextual Tip Surfaces. Contextual surfaces should stay compact and text-first, showing only the short caution needed in that itinerary context, with Source Markers only when provenance is needed. The full explanation remains in the Tips area or shared detail view. This keeps the Tips area readable as local guidance, while giving travelers timely cautions at the moment they are reviewing a meal, transport segment, religious site, market, evening area, or weather-sensitive activity.

Showing tips only as a separate themed guide was rejected because important cautions could be missed during itinerary arrangement; duplicating them directly into itinerary content was rejected because it would blur informational guidance with scheduled travel material.
