# Keep contextual tips short

When a Travel Tip is strongly related to an Itinerary Day or Itinerary Block, Landi may show it as a Contextual Tip Surface near that itinerary context. The contextual surface should show only the short caution needed at that moment.

The full explanation, source state, freshness state, and broader actions remain in the Canonical Tip in the Tips area or in the shared detail view. Contextual tips reference the same underlying Travel Tip and should not duplicate the full content into the itinerary.

Putting full tip explanations beside itinerary blocks was rejected because it would make the timeline and detail surroundings too noisy. The user needs the immediate caution in context, then can open the full tip when they want more detail.
