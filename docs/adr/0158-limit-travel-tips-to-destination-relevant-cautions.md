# Limit Travel Tips to destination-relevant cautions

The Landi-native planning Skill should generate Travel Tips only when they are tied to the destination, local culture, local infrastructure, trip context, or a concrete itinerary situation. Good Travel Tips cover things like etiquette, transport habits, payment norms, safety, language, weather, dress, taboos, scams, tourist traps, and common local mistakes.

Generic travel inspiration, broad life advice, motivational copy, and obvious universal reminders should not appear in Tips.

This keeps Tips useful as a local-friction surface and makes contextual tip surfacing beside itinerary blocks meaningful instead of noisy.
