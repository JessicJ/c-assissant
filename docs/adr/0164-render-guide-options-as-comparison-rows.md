# Render guide options as comparison rows

Guide Option Comparison should default to compact Option Comparison Rows or a table-like layout. Each row represents one concrete dining place, purchase place, or experience offering and prioritizes Comparison Facts such as name, area, distance or route fit, hours or source state, price or effort, reservation or queue friction, and action controls.

Images may appear as small supporting thumbnails when useful, but peer options should not default to large image-led cards. The larger editorial presentation belongs to Guide Cards and Guide Value Explanation, not the comparison list.

Large card stacks were rejected because they make comparison slower and make peer options compete visually. The detail-panel comparison job is to help the traveler choose among concrete options efficiently.
