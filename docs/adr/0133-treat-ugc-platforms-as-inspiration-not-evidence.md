# Treat UGC platforms as inspiration not evidence

Landi may use social, UGC, short-video, image-board, review, and travel-community platforms as optional Inspiration Sources for guide themes, visual context, local products, dining ideas, shopping ideas, and local experiences. UGC is one Source Channel among many, not a required dependency. These sources can shape what Landi investigates and how guide material is presented, but they cannot by themselves verify opening times, reservation requirements, official rules, route reliability, safety claims, prices, or execution-sensitive facts.

Treating UGC as ordinary evidence was rejected because it would make plans feel current and vivid at first, but would blur popularity with reliability and could cause unstable or non-authoritative claims to drive itinerary and execution decisions.

Missing UGC or social Inspiration Sources do not block complete plan generation when reliable maps, official, merchant, review, image search, or structured provider facts are sufficient. Landi should degrade to a more conservative reliable-guide presentation rather than failing the trip.
