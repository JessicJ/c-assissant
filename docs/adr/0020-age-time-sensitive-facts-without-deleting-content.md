# Age time-sensitive facts without deleting travel content

Landi records when Time-sensitive Facts were verified and rechecks them near their relevant travel date. A fact becoming stale changes its Evidence Status and may create an Action Requirement, but it does not delete its place, itinerary entry, Trip Blueprint, or Plan; future uncertainty is handled through explicit revalidation rather than generation failure.

## Consequences

Evidence records need retrieval times and fact categories suitable for recheck policy. Execution may schedule revalidation work after Plan approval, while the Plan Workspace continues to show the last known fact and its current status.
