# Separate Experience Themes from Offerings

Landi will model Experience Themes separately from Experience Offerings. An Experience Theme explains the local cultural or activity concept, suitable travelers, timing, and practical considerations. An Experience Offering identifies a concrete schedulable activity, venue, provider, event, route, or place where that theme can be experienced.

One Experience Theme may link to multiple Experience Offerings. When multiple offerings exist, Web should show an Experience Offering Comparison with location, duration, suitable itinerary area or day part, booking need, seasonal fit, accessibility, etiquette or risk notes, source state, and nearby itinerary links when available.

A local-experience item can become a schedulable Itinerary Block only when the traveler or generated itinerary selects a specific Experience Offering, not merely a vague experience theme. Theme-only experience ideas remain informational guide content.
