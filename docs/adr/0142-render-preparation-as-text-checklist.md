# Render preparation as text checklist

Landi will render pre-departure preparation as grouped text-only checklist content, with expandable written details, due timing, source-backed status, and completion controls. Preparation items do not require images and should not use image-led Guide Card presentation.

Preparation items should also follow a relevance rule: they must materially affect trip readiness, legality, access, booking use, payment, connectivity, weather fit, health, safety, transport setup, or destination-specific procedure. Generic life advice and broad wellness suggestions should not be generated as preparation tasks unless tied to a concrete travel requirement.

Image-led preparation was rejected because preparation is primarily an execution and verification job. Text checklists make required actions easier to scan, complete, and connect to reminders or official evidence.
