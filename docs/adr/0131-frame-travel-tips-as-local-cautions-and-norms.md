# Frame Travel Tips as local cautions and norms

Landi will present Travel Tips as local customs, cautions, and practical norms, including etiquette, transport habits, payment norms, safety, language, weather, dress, taboos, scams, tourist traps, and common local mistakes. Travel Tips use compact text-first cards and do not require images. Claims that need provenance can expose sources through a compact Source Marker.

Travel Tips must follow a relevance rule: each tip should be tied to the destination, local culture, local infrastructure, trip context, or a concrete itinerary situation. Generic travel inspiration, broad life advice, motivational copy, and obvious universal reminders should not be generated as tips.

This keeps tips focused on helping the traveler avoid friction rather than turning the area into a generic FAQ, image-led guide, travel essay, or duplicate of itinerary and preparation content.
