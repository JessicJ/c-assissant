# Organize guide material by traveler themes

Landi will organize itinerary guide material primarily by traveler-facing themes or experiences, with specific places and purchase options attached inside those groups. This makes attractions, dining, shopping, and local experiences easier to browse as planning choices rather than presenting the traveler with an undifferentiated place list.

Place-first organization remains useful for maps and search, but was rejected as the default guide presentation because it hides the reason a traveler might choose an item and makes shopping or dining guidance feel like a directory instead of planning material.
