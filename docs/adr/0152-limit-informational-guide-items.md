# Limit Informational Guide Items

Landi-native generation should prioritize Schedulable Itinerary Objects and keep Informational Guide Items limited to high-value context that helps travelers understand local food, products, culture, customs, or experience choices.

This selectivity is qualitative, not a fixed ratio or quota per destination or guide type. The amount of backup material should follow trip context, evidence quality, and usefulness.

Large volumes of theme-only background content were rejected because they make the workspace feel rich while weakening its ability to support concrete plan review and adjustment. Informational material should earn its place by improving traveler understanding, not by filling the guide surface.
