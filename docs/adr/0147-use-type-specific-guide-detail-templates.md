# Use type-specific guide detail templates

Landi will render attraction, dining, shopping, and local-experience details inside the same Workspace Side Panel shell, but each content type uses a type-specific Guide Detail Template.

Attraction details focus on a concrete Attraction Place, visit meaning, duration, practical notes, and related places. Dining details focus on Local Food, local food context, Dining Place Comparison when multiple concrete Dining Places or food areas exist, reservation or queue expectations, dietary notes, and meal fit. Shopping details focus on Local Products, local product context, Purchase Place Comparison when multiple concrete Purchase Places exist, price or quality or convenience signals, and tax refund, payment, or shipping notes. Local-experience details focus on Experience Themes, local context, Experience Offering Comparison when multiple concrete offerings exist, suitable traveler, duration, booking need, seasonal fit, etiquette, and risk notes.

The detail template may use Guide Option Comparison when multiple concrete dining places, purchase places, or experience offerings are useful. These options should be presented as peer choices, not as lower-status candidates. Guide Option Comparison should default to compact Option Comparison Rows or a table-like layout instead of large image-led cards for every option. The Skill should not be required to reduce these decisions to one universal best option before the traveler has compared route fit, price or quality signals, reservation or queue friction, timing, source state, and personal fit. Web may use Neutral Option Ordering to reduce browsing effort without labeling one option as best or recommended.

Guide details may include Guide Value Explanation: why the traveler might care about the place, food, product, or experience. This is content value explanation, not Per-block Arrangement Rationale. It should not be required to explain why an item was scheduled on a particular day or time.

One generic detail template was rejected because it would either hide important category-specific fields or force every detail view to carry irrelevant labels.
