# Do not block generation on missing UGC sources

Landi will not block complete plan generation merely because social, UGC, short-video, image-board, or travel-community sources are unavailable. UGC is one optional Source Channel among many, not a required planning dependency. When maps, official, merchant, review, image search, or other structured facts are sufficient, Landi should still generate a complete recommended itinerary, preparation, and tips, while presenting guide material with less social or lifestyle texture.
