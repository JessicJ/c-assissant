# Limit preparation to travel-relevant items

The Landi-native planning Skill should generate Preparation Checklist items only when they materially affect the trip's readiness, legality, access, booking use, payment, connectivity, weather fit, health, safety, transport setup, or destination-specific procedure.

Generic life advice, broad wellness suggestions, and filler tasks should not appear in Preparation unless tied to a concrete travel requirement. Examples of rejected generic items include buying an unrelated cup, organizing photos, or simply reminding the traveler to rest early.

This keeps Preparation useful as a travel readiness surface rather than letting it become a noisy personal to-do list.
