# Limit first Execution Action Cards to movement, food, and weather

The first Travel Execution Capability slice will expose Movement, Nearby Food, and Weather Impact Action Cards. Lodging-area recommendation, entry preparation, payment setup, insurance, SIM or eSIM setup, and destination guide content remain outside this slice because they belong to planning or pre-departure preparation rather than immediate on-trip action.

## Considered Options

- Include lodging and preparation cards in the first slice. This would make the product feel broader, but it would blur execution assistance with planning and destination-specific preparation.
- Limit the first slice to movement, nearby food, and weather. These needs map directly to Landi's existing location sharing, Google Maps, Google Weather, route facts, reminders, and handoff boundaries.
