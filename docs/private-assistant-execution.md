# Confirmed-plan personal assistance

This replaces the user-initiated-only movement scope of the original on-trip execution slice. The traveler approves a Plan version once; its structured itinerary then drives proactive assistance. Generating a Plan and importing a Plan are upstream adapters, not different execution engines. A future importer must produce the same structured Plan/Blueprint contract.

## User-facing voice

All traveler messages in the execution loop should sound like a calm personal assistant speaking directly to the traveler. Lead with the next useful action or a short acknowledgement, use everyday language, and keep each message focused on the current stop. Preserve destination names, appointment times and genuinely useful preparation advice. Keep internal state, reason codes, leases, defaults, provider payloads and diagnostic summaries in logs/API data, never in traveler copy. Missing data should produce an actionable question or fallback only when it affects the traveler's decision; unknown luggage, for example, becomes a conditional reminder to choose a larger car when carrying suitcases. Do not invent confirmed arrival, completed activities, bookings, prices or live availability. Explain booking confirmation at the booking handoff, and location privacy at the location request, without repeating disclaimers throughout the conversation. Test messages may briefly disclose simulation, but should not expose the test harness implementation. Use the traveler's chosen language consistently when localization is available; full localization of the existing English flow remains separate work.

Arrival must acknowledge the destination in the chat and explain when the assistant will check back. Repeated arrival taps preserve the original start time and do not duplicate the acknowledgement. Progress questions invite "finished" or "more time" rather than announcing an internal duration threshold. Finishing the last activity receives a warm completion message. Receipt failures stay in the bounded delivery-retry mechanism, with a diagnostic failure code stored internally.

## Execution behavior

The explicitly designated one-day simulation can pin its calendar date in the local acceptance manifest (`simulation.pinLocalDate`) to continue acceptance across midnight. This applies only to the exact test Plan ID/version, matching itinerary dates and matching timezone; real progress/delay timers remain unchanged. Production Plans retain their local-day guard.

Ride-app handoffs are separate from distance/weather recommendations. Dated official city coverage records provide positive evidence, but a missing city or expired record never removes a car-app entry point or proves absence. Unknown cities in a known provider country retain its app action; unknown countries retain Uber/Grab app checks. Only explicit, applicable, current first-party negative evidence can exclude a provider. Motorbike alternatives still require matching product evidence. The curated snapshot is not a live driver or pickup-zone check; airports, long journeys and restricted pickup areas require confirmation in the app. Refresh official sources and checked/expiry dates when revalidating evidence.

- Compile all scheduled daily blocks (including meals, lodging and transfers); legacy stops remain supported. Optional, replacement and excluded blocks are not active itinerary activities.
- An empty outbound departure day is materialized from the confirmed origin airport into preparation, airport transport, check-in/security and departure steps. The following empty in-flight day receives an in-flight step. Resolve the airport identity and city before publishing; do not infer bookings, terminals or pickup addresses. Times retained from legacy prose are marked for ticket rechecking.
- An outbound departure step also compiles a preparation reminder for the previous calendar day, at the existing suggested 09:00 origin-local prompt. This does not extend the displayed trip dates. Airport transport remains untimed until flight/check-in and pickup details support a departure calculation; it follows the normal feedback and transport-card flow.
- Departure preparation includes date-matched origin/destination forecasts and a packing checklist, with rain and warm-layer suggestions derived only from available trip-date forecasts. Current weather must not substitute for an unavailable future forecast. Confirm-flight and pickup-address buttons collect Plan-version-scoped details; geocoded planning pickup addresses require an explicit second confirmation and support advance departure estimates only. Preparing an airport ride always requests a fresh one-time location, even when a planning address exists. The movement card recalculates the leave-by time and expected airport arrival from that shared pickup, without storing its coordinates in departure details. Location requests expire after 15 minutes; designated test Plans continue to use simulated pickup locations. The original itinerary clock remains unchanged.
- Airport timing subtracts the confirmed airport arrival lead, a traffic-aware driving estimate, and a pickup/traffic buffer of at least 30 minutes (or 30% of the driving estimate). A supplied check-in cutoff adds a further 30-minute margin when stricter than the airport lead. LAX international departures use a minimum 180-minute airport lead, following [LAWA guidance](https://www.lawa.org/news-releases/2025/break-free-travel-stress-lax-enhanced-technology-and-pro-tips-smooth-july-fourth); other airports require an explicit lead. Airline requirements may be stricter. Refresh the route every 15 minutes within 48 hours of the confirmed flight; mark an older estimate when refreshing fails.
- Send ride preparation 30 minutes before the calculated leave time, then a distinct leave-now reminder. Neither unfinished checklist feedback nor snooze may postpone the airport deadline. Early flights can trigger airport travel on the previous local calendar day. Arrival feedback stops further departure prompts. These are conservative route estimates, not a guarantee against missed flights; terminal-specific access, live flight changes and airline policy lookup are not yet automated.
- Keep compiled activities, progress, timezone overrides and delivery attempts in `execution_assistant_states`, scoped to Plan ID and version. Schema version 21 adds this table without changing existing itinerary content.
- Confirmation initializes execution through the existing active-plan monitoring entry point. Future trips compile immediately but do not send reminders before the appropriate local day.
- Timed activities remind at their time minus verified travel duration and 10-minute buffer. Without verified travel duration, send a 30-minute preparation reminder; never claim an exact departure time from unknown route facts.
- Untimed activities follow explicit completion or skip feedback. The first untimed activity has a disclosed suggested morning prompt at 09:00 local time. This is an execution default, not a stored appointment.
- Telegram buttons accept start/arrival, finish, skip, a 15-minute delay and transport preparation. Estimated activity durations prompt a question rather than automatically completing a step.
- Transport requests are bound to the selected activity and approved Plan version. One-time location sharing generates the existing local transport card. Estimated travel timing prompts an arrival question, never an inferred completed arrival.
- An explicit outbound airport arrival receives a practical acknowledgement and completes the airport-transfer step once that message is delivered. The next check-in/security reminder follows immediately when earlier preparation is completed; repeated arrival taps do not reopen the transfer or resend the acknowledgement. Airline, terminal, bag-drop, gate and boarding-time checks use traveler-facing checklist copy without inventing ticket details.
- In-flight activities remain visible in the itinerary but never send proactive reminders or require completion for the itinerary to finish. A single international-arrival card is due at the planned local arrival time, includes date-matched destination weather, asks for landing confirmation, and offers ride preparation for the next scheduled destination. A ready-for-ride action explicitly confirms arrival readiness and requests a fresh one-time pickup for that destination. Landing-only confirmation opens an immigration/baggage checklist without hourly follow-ups. Legacy arrival cards and automatic location follow-ups are disabled for Plans with structured international-arrival activities to avoid duplicate messages.
- Delay pauses reminders for 15 minutes. Fixed itinerary appointments and bookings are not rewritten. Delivery leases and up to three attempts preserve restart recovery and successful-send deduplication.
- Missing city timezone produces a prompt instead of guessing. `/timezone City | IANA/Zone` supplies a Plan-scoped execution override. Provider-derived zones are persisted for restart continuity. Arrival monitoring can reuse the resolved zone for the arrival day.
- `GET /api/plans/:planId/execution` exposes compiled activities, gaps and execution progress; drafts expose an inert compilation and no running progress.

## Boundaries and limits

Approval is required before execution state can be written. A replacement draft does not cancel the currently executing Plan; activation switches the executing version and invalidates earlier activity controls. GPS shares are not stored in this state. An explicitly confirmed airport pickup address and its geocoded coordinates are retained only in the Plan-version-scoped departure details, not in the Profile.

Existing provider handoffs remain recommendations and external-app entry points. No booking, payment, ride dispatch or background GPS tracking is performed. Grab remains manual handoff; Uber availability is evaluated at the verified pickup, with the selected destination prefilled. A rural destination must not hide Uber for a supported city pickup. Reverse geocoding resolves pickup country and city; a named-address fallback must match the shared coordinates. Unknown pickups never borrow geography from the plan or destination. Long journeys ask the traveler to check driver acceptance and fare in the app, with an arranged driver as a fallback.

Timezone resolution initially reuses current geocoding/weather capabilities; the explicit override recovers unsupported provider coverage. Rows lacking a date, city or activity identity are exposed as compilation gaps rather than fabricated. Untimed activities with no duration use a disclosed 60-minute estimated duration for the progress question. Cross-day unresolved activities are retained in state; today's itinerary can start without claiming yesterday's activities completed.

Feedback is currently button-driven. Natural-language completion/delay parsing, custom snooze durations, rescheduling fixed commitments, and a Web progress-control surface remain future work.

## Validation

Run server and storage tests. `execution-assistant.test.ts` uses independent SQLite storage and captured Telegram messages to test approval, timed and untimed progression, arrival questions, delay, retry limits, stale controls, timezone recovery and restart persistence. `server-lifecycle.test.ts` invokes the real monitoring service through controlled scheduler intervals and verifies delivery deduplication after restart. Movement-card tests verify that explicit activity transport does not fall back to an obsolete first itinerary stop.

These checks do not prove real-phone delivery or provider availability during a real trip. Use an isolated same-day itinerary for device acceptance, with all test notifications explicitly marked, without changing a real travel Plan.

## Device acceptance simulation

The local device-session manifest can opt one exact TEST Plan ID/version into a simulated pickup. Its transport prompt offers a simulated Los Angeles pickup button instead of GPS sharing. Production Plans cannot use this override. Card preparation sends a progress acknowledgement and has a 45-second preparation deadline with an explicit recoverable failure reply. Test cards identify the pickup as simulated.

Interactive arrival acceptance uses a separate backup database of the same confirmed Plan. Namespaced `at:` controls route to that isolated state, bind to the paired chat and original Plan ID/version, and expire after 24 hours. Simulated airport pickup is an explicit button choice; GPS is never requested by this test. Original execution progress is untouched. The test clock is injected per assistant instance rather than changing the server clock. Restarting the server reloads the isolated state; a Plan version change invalidates the test card.

Confirmed arrival completes a transfer after its acknowledgement is delivered, allowing the destination activity to follow immediately. Hotel luggage-drop prompts ask about room readiness and luggage storage without assuming a room or reservation is confirmed. The luggage-ready button receives a deduplicated chat acknowledgement and the next planned activity/time; failed acknowledgement delivery can retry up to three times.

Meal placeholders without a selected restaurant offer up to three nearby options around the preceding planned location, filter provider-reported closures, and link walking directions from that explicitly named origin. Introductions use sourced restaurant categories or short Google Maps editorial summaries, available price tiers and vegetarian-option facts; website links allow menu review. Recommendation reasons use verified route convenience, without inventing signature dishes, ambience, exact prices or reservations. Walking durations come from route queries; straight-line distance is labeled as approximate when a route cannot be checked. Meal start and completion are explicit controls. Existing selected restaurant activities retain their destination and movement flow. Opening hours, menus and dietary needs require traveler review; the recommendation never claims a table or booking is confirmed.

### Place transport buttons

Place cards follow the plan's explicit transport choice. `driving` means the user has chosen their own car and shows Google Maps driving directions. `taxi` means ride hail: meal suggestions let the user choose a restaurant, then request a fresh, once-only pickup before producing locally verified Uber/Grab actions. Selected restaurants are scoped to the execution activity and plan version; they do not rewrite the confirmed itinerary. Available ride-app buttons take precedence over map links. Walking and verified public transport show their corresponding routes. Incomplete ride coverage keeps useful app entry points without claiming confirmed local service; a local taxi remains an alternative. Interactive acceptance uses the session's simulated pickup, including the hotel for lunch, and never requests the user's real location.

Restaurant recommendations include up to three sourced menu picks and an official menu button when the menu can be read. `RestaurantMenuFact` carries the source URL, check time and individual dish evidence category; ordinary menu dishes must not be labeled signatures or bestsellers. The current official HTML adapters support Auckland Amano and Ortolana, with restaurant name, city and official website checks. Fetches have a six-second deadline and size limit; failures leave the restaurant and ride choices usable. Lunch does not use a dinner-only menu. Unsupported menus keep the restaurant website link without invented dishes. Successful menus are cached for six hours; the copy explains that seasonal dishes can change.

### Quiet visits

An explicit transfer arrival at a sightseeing destination also starts the following visit at the same place. It sends one acknowledgement without a second Start button. During an active visit, ordinary later itinerary reminders wait for the visitor; airport departure deadlines retain their independent triggers. The assistant asks about the next stop ten minutes before the estimated visit ends (or one quarter of a short visit). The clock never marks a visit finished. Ready for the next stop explicitly finishes the visit and prepares the next journey; Stay 15 more minutes delays the question. Existing confirmed transfer arrivals resume this behavior without repeating the welcome.

Arrival button confirmations for sightseeing visits, ordinary transfers and hotel rest update execution state and answer the callback only. They send no duplicate chat acknowledgement. Transfer arrival starts the same-place visit or completes an overnight hotel arrival directly. Actionable airport check-in and luggage instructions remain separate next-step reminders.

Before a sightseeing visit, the departure reminder includes a concise saved-place introduction, up to three saved highlights or relevant tour/daytime/evening details, useful preparation and booking reminders, and the official venue link when present. Transfers to a sightseeing stop include the same guide. Estimated visit durations are internal scheduling inputs, not instructions limiting the traveler's exploration; the guide does not announce a time Landi has set aside. Actual booked tour or entry times remain useful factual information. Transport controls retain the user's selected mode. Arrival and near-end cards do not repeat the introduction. Missing guide content is omitted; this renderer does not invent highlights, opening hours, prices or confirmed bookings, and saved content is not presented as freshly verified live information.


### Evidence and ride app actions

A missing city, unknown pickup country, failed location lookup or expired positive coverage record never means a provider does not operate. Known-country car providers remain as app actions outside the catalogued cities. Without country evidence, offer Uber and Grab app entry points for checking the supplied pickup; do not assert local coverage or live vehicles. `coverageStatus` separates sourced city support from `check_in_app`, but diagnostic statuses are not shown to the traveler. Motorbike services still need positive product evidence. Only explicit first-party negative records with an applicable country/city, source URL, reason and current checked/expiry window can exclude a provider. Uber's published Southeast Asia transition to Grab is recorded as negative evidence. Refresh the evidence when it expires. Deduplicate provider buttons. Actual car availability, driver acceptance and fare are determined in the selected app. Arrival and movement handoffs use pickup geography, never destination or overall trip geography.


Arrival buttons provide persistent feedback by updating the original card to `✓ Arrived` and removing that activity's delay button, while preserving ride/map links. This happens only after arrival is saved for the matching plan version and day. Repeated taps preserve the activity timer and can refresh the same card. No duplicate chat acknowledgement is sent for a leisure visit. A card-edit failure must not undo confirmed arrival. Telegram callback message identity and inline markup pass through the production and isolated acceptance paths.


### Obstruction-only monitoring

After plan confirmation, journey monitoring runs every five minutes and venue status checks every thirty minutes. These checks do not replace normal execution-node reminders. They remain silent unless sourced evidence changes what the traveler should do: fresh disruptive weather at an active or imminent outdoor place; traffic that creates a meaningful risk of missing a scheduled arrival; changed/cancelled saved bookings or overlapping confirmed reservations; or specific unresolved preparation for tomorrow during 18:00–22:00 local time. Next-day forecasts must actually contain tomorrow's date. Hotel/restaurant/museum visits do not receive ordinary rain alerts. GPS is not read; pre-departure traffic estimates describe the route from the last explicitly confirmed itinerary place, never a claimed live user ETA. During a recorded journey, the monitor does not invent a remaining travel time from the original pickup. Explicit transport preference or a verified planned route selects the mode. Airport transfers use the calculated airport-arrival target instead of flight takeoff time.

Alerts store fingerprints and delivery status in plan/version-scoped execution state. The same issue is quiet across restarts; materially changed impact may produce an update. Successful normal checks clear a problem so a later recurrence can notify; provider failures do not clear known problems or create warnings. Failed Telegram sends retry at most three times. Appointment monitoring reads saved/imported booking records and their changes; it is not a live airline or reservation-provider connection. A closure must match the planned venue's name and city/coordinates. Neither monitoring nor an alert changes bookings or the approved itinerary.

Rain-easing guidance uses two consecutive contiguous low-rain hourly forecast intervals and states an approximate local-time forecast, never a precise rain-stop guarantee. Daily totals cannot produce a rain-stop time. API reference: https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/weather/hourly-forecast


An explicit `Ready for the next stop` action records a version-scoped request to present the next same-day activity immediately, even when its original reminder time is later. It does not change the itinerary time or mark that next activity started. Meal suggestions without a selected place are included. Automatic clock-driven checks still respect the scheduled time. The request survives restart and delivery retry, and clears when the traveler acts on that next activity. Repeated taps do not duplicate an already delivered card.
